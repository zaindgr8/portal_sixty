Sixty AI does

  • Reads the Form A (PDF or photos of pages) and fills every field it can find.
  • Shows where each value came from, with the quote and page.
  • Labels rooms, picks a cover, suggests a photo order and flags weak photos.

  • On portals without an API connection: press the portal's own Submit after the extension fills the form.

Before you start

One-time setup

Do this once per agency, and the Chrome extension part once per agent. It takes about five minutes.

A · Save your agency and agent numbers Admin

  1. Open PropTech → Portal Settings.
  2. Under Agency & agents, enter the agency ORN (RERA office number).
  3. For each agent, enter their BRN, the phone and email buyers should see, and their Property Finder user ID if you have it.
  4. Press Save numbers. Every new listing is filled in with these automatically.
✦If an agent's BRN is missing, the AI will ask for it on each listing. Saving it here means it never has to.
PropTech → Portal Settings

B · Install the Sixty Chrome extension Each agent · Google Chrome on a computer

  1. Open PropTech → Chrome extension and press Download. Unzip the file: you get a folder called sixty-extension.
  2. In Chrome go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Press Load unpacked, click once on the sixty-extension folder and press Select. Chrome only accepts the folder, not the zip.
  4. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Sixty Auto Listing.
  5. Back in Sixty press Get pairing code → Copy. Click the Sixty icon, paste the code and press Connect.
The extension, ready to be paired
!Treat the pairing code like a password. It lasts 90 days and works only for you. Lost a laptop? Press Disconnect and every old code stops working.

What to upload

The more the AI can read, the less you type. Only the Form A and five photos are required.

ItemWhat the AI uses it forFormat
Form A / listing formRequiredSale or rent, type, location, building, unit, floor, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, price, the owner's details, Form A number and expiry.PDF, or a photo of each page (JPG, PNG, HEIC). Up to 25 MB each.
Property photos5 to 50Room labels, cover choice, photo order, amenities it can see (pool, gym, view), and quality warnings.JPG, PNG, HEIC or WebP. Up to 25 MB each.
Title deed or OqoodOptionalConfirms the unit, size and owner. Documents win over notes for legal details.PDF or photos.
Floor planOptionalLayout and size checks; can be published with the photos.PDF or image.
Voice noteOptionalRecord it in the browser or upload one. Say what makes the home special: upgrades, view, vacancy, viewing times. Used for the description.Up to 3 minutes. m4a, mp3, webm, ogg, wav.
NotesOptionalSame as the voice note, typed.Text.

Photos that portals accept

✓ Do

  • Landscape, bright, level shots.
  • At least 800 px on the short side (most phones are far above this).
  • One clear exterior or view photo you'd be happy to see as the cover.
  • Every main room at least once.

✕ Avoid

  • Watermarks, logos, phone numbers or text on the photo: portals reject these.
  • People, number plates or personal items.
  • Screenshots and collages.
  • Very dark or blurry shots. Sixty flags them so you can swap them.
🔒Owner details stay private. The owner's name, phone and Emirates ID / passport from the Form A are stored encrypted, shown only to agency admins, and never sent to any portal or to the Chrome extension.

1Step one

Upload and let the AI work

One screen, one button. The AI takes about a minute and you can leave the page while it works.

  1. Go to PropTech → New Listing.
  2. Add Form A: drop the PDF, or several photos of its pages.
  3. Add photos: drop 5 to 50 at once. Each shows its own progress bar. Tap the ☆ to choose the cover yourself, or let the AI choose.
  4. Optional: add a title deed, Oqood or floor plan, record a voice note, or type notes.
  5. Press Read & write listing. The button lights up once the Form A and five photos are in.
✦Several properties? Press Add another listing to fill up to 10 cards on one screen, then start them all together.
PropTech → New Listing

3Step three

Photos and copy

The AI has already sorted the photos and written both languages. Adjust anything you like.

Photos

  • Each photo has a room label.
  • The COVER is the first photo portals show. Tap ☆ on another photo to change it.
  • Use the arrows to reorder.
  • The bin removes a photo from the listing (you can restore it).
  • Warnings such as low resolution or a watermark appear on the photo.

English and Arabic copy

  • Switch between English and العربية.
  • Pick a tone: Professional, Luxury or Investor, then press Regenerate.
  • Edit the text directly if you prefer.
  • The counter shows the length portals expect: 750 to 1,500 characters for descriptions.
  • The AI writes only from verified details and your notes. It never adds phone numbers, emails, links or emoji, which portals reject.
✦Added something to your notes or voice transcript after the AI wrote the copy? Press Regenerate so it's included.

4Step four

Trakheesi permit and checklist

Dubai requires a valid Trakheesi advertising permit on every listing. Sixty helps you apply, then locks publishing until the permit is in.

  1. Press Copy permit application data. Everything the Trakheesi form asks for is copied in one go. Download bundle gives you the Form A plus a details PDF to attach.
  2. Apply on Trakheesi as usual.
  3. Back in Sixty, enter the Trakheesi permit number and Permit expiry.
  4. Check the Agency ORN and Agent BRN (filled from Portal Settings).
!Sixty never makes up a permit. Only a real Trakheesi permit can be entered. An expired permit blocks publishing until it's renewed.

The pre-publish checklist

Before the Publish button unlocks, every line must be green:

✓Valid Trakheesi permit
✓Required details filled in
✓At least 5 good photos and a clean cover
✓Title and description fit the portals' rules

Each red line has a Fix link that jumps to the field.

6Step six

Post with the Chrome extension

For portals not connected by API or feed. Sixty fills the portal's own form and photos in your Chrome; you check and press the portal's Submit.

Click the Sixty icon: approved listings wait here
  1. Click the Sixty icon in Chrome's toolbar. Your approved listing is under Waiting to post.
  2. Press Post on Property Finder (or Bayut). The portal opens in a new tab.
  3. Sign in if asked and open the portal's Add listing page. The Sixty panel appears at the bottom right with your listing.
  4. Press Fill this page. Fields Sixty filled get a green outline.
  5. On each step of the portal's form press Fill this page again, then the portal's Next. On the photos step press Add photos: they go in cover first.
The Sixty panel on the portal's Add listing page
  1. Anything Sixty couldn't fill has a Copy button. Pick the Location from the portal's suggestion list yourself, and type the unit number.
  2. Check everything and press the portal's own Submit. Sixty never submits for you.
  3. Paste the new listing's link into the panel and press I've submitted it. In Sixty the listing now shows PF · Live.

Tracking and changes

Every listing shows where it's live, and every action, by a person, the AI or the extension, is recorded.

Listing status

DraftNot sent to the AI yet.
AI workingReading and writing. About a minute.
Needs reviewReady for you to check.
ApprovedYou published; on its way to the portals.
PublishedLive on at least one portal.
FailedThe AI step couldn't finish. Fill in by hand or try again.

Portal status

QueuedWaiting to be sent, or waiting in the extension.
SubmittedThe portal is checking it.
LiveOn the portal. Tap view to open it.
RejectedThe portal said no. The reason is shown in plain words; fix and publish again.
ExpiredThe permit or listing ran out.
A live listing: Update or Unpublish any time

Changing a live listing

Edit the fields, photos or copy, then press Update next to the portal. To take it down, press Unpublish. If you posted it with the extension or by hand, remove it on the portal too, then press I took it down.

The record

The Portal event log (admins) shows each step. The Audit log records who published, changed the permit or unpublished.

Help

Questions and rules

The AI got a field wrong.
Change it. Your value is marked as yours and the AI won't overwrite it, even if you regenerate the copy.
The checklist says the description is too short.
Portals want 750 to 1,500 characters. Add a detail to your notes and press Regenerate, or add a sentence yourself.
Publish is greyed out.
Look at the pre-publish checklist: each red line has a Fix link. Most often it's the permit or the BRN.
The listing is stuck on "AI working".
Keep the listing page open for a minute. If it says the AI step failed, fill in the fields by hand, or ask your admin to look at it.
A photo failed to upload.
Check it's a JPG, PNG, HEIC or WebP under 25 MB, and try again. Large files upload straight to secure storage, so a weak connection is the usual cause.
The Sixty panel doesn't appear on the portal.
Check the extension is paired (click the icon: it shows your name). Then open the listing from Post on… in the extension so the panel knows which listing to use.
"Fill this page" missed some fields.
Portals change their forms. Use the Copy buttons for the rest. Sixty updates its fill rules centrally, with no reinstall needed.
The extension says it isn't connected.
Your code expired or was disconnected. Get a new code under PropTech → Chrome extension and paste it in.
Can I ask questions about my listings?
Yes. Press Ask Sixty (⌘J) and ask, for example, "Which listings still need a permit?"

The rules Sixty always follows

1A person approves every listing. Nothing reaches a portal until someone in your agency presses Publish.
2Permit first. No valid Trakheesi permit, no publishing.
3Owner data is private. Never published, never sent to portals or the extension, shown only to admins.
4Each agency is separate. No agency can see another's listings, files or numbers.
